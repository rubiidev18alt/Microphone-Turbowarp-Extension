# Branding assets

- `microphone-icon.svg`: editable source for the 128 × 128 palette/block icon.
- `microphone-icon-128.png`, `microphone-icon-48.png`, `microphone-icon-32.png`: raster exports for previews or platforms that require PNG.
- `extension-banner.svg`: editable 960 × 300 extension banner.
- `extension-banner.png`: PNG export of the banner.

The design uses a bright blue background, white microphone, and sound waves to
match the approachable, high-contrast visual language of Scratch extensions.
It is an original icon inspired by that style rather than a copied Scratch asset.

## Suggested title and description

**Title:** Microphone Recorder

**Description:** Record audio from your microphone and play back your latest take in TurboWarp.

**Banner tagline:** Record your voice. Play it back.
